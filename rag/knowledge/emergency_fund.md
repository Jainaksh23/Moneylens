# Building and Sizing an Emergency Fund

**Author**: MoneyLens team  
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-15  
**Category**: Liquidity & Risk Management  

## Purpose of an Emergency Fund
An emergency fund is a dedicated, highly liquid cash buffer reserved exclusively for unplanned, non-negotiable financial shocks. Its primary objective is capital preservation and rapid accessibility, preventing the need to liquidate long-term investments (like equity mutual funds) at a market loss or resort to high-interest personal loans or credit card debt.

## Common Genuine Emergencies
- Unexpected job loss, furlough, or transition periods between employment.
- Critical hospitalisation or medical emergencies not fully covered by cashless health insurance.
- Urgent, unavoidable vehicle or home structural repairs.
- Immediate financial support required for dependent family members.

## Calculating the Target Size
The recommended emergency fund size depends on income stability, number of dependents, and monthly non-discretionary commitments:
- **Salaried with Stable Employment (Dual Income / No Dependents)**: 3 to 6 months of mandatory living expenses.
- **Single-Earner Household with Dependents**: 6 to 9 months of mandatory living expenses.
- **Freelancers, Contractors, or Business Owners (Variable Income)**: 9 to 12 months of mandatory living expenses.

*Mandatory Living Expenses* = Rent + Utilities + Groceries + Children's Education + Health/Term Insurance Premiums + Ongoing Loan EMIs.

## Where to Park the Emergency Fund
Avoid locking emergency funds in volatile assets (stocks, equity mutual funds, crypto) or illiquid instruments (real estate, long-term locked deposits). Instead, utilize a multi-tier allocation:
1. **Tier 1 (Instant Liquidity - 20-30%)**: High-interest savings bank account with instant ATM/UPI withdrawal access.
2. **Tier 2 (Same-Day / T+1 Liquidity - 70-80%)**: Sweep-in fixed deposits (auto-FD), overnight funds, or low-duration liquid mutual funds offering instant redemption facilities (up to ₹50,000 per day under SEBI rules).

## Rules of Discipline
- **Replenish Immediately**: If any portion is withdrawn for an emergency, pause discretionary goals and prioritize refilling the buffer.
- **Not for Planned Discretionary Costs**: Festive shopping, vacations, and new device launches must be funded through sinking funds, never the emergency reserve.
