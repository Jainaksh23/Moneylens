# Gold Allocation and Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB)

**Author**: MoneyLens team  
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-15  
**Category**: Asset Allocation & Precious Metals  

## Role of Gold in an Indian Portfolio
In India, gold holds profound cultural and emotional significance. Financially, gold acts as a non-correlated hedge against equity market turbulence, currency depreciation (rupee vs USD), and macroeconomic inflation.

Most prudent asset allocation frameworks suggest allocating between **5% and 10%** of an investor's total liquid portfolio to gold. Exceeding 15% is generally sub-optimal because gold generates no underlying cash flow, dividends, or industrial earnings growth; its price is dictated primarily by macro sentiment and currency movements.

## Ways to Hold Gold
1. **Physical Gold (Jewellery & Coins)**:
   - Disadvantages: High making charges (8% to 25%), storage/locker fees, theft risk, GST (3%), and purity deduction during resale. Physical jewellery is an emotional consumption item, not an efficient investment.
2. **Gold ETFs and Mutual Funds**:
   - Backed by 99.5% pure physical gold held in vaults.
   - Traded on stock exchanges with high liquidity and zero making charges.
   - Taxed at applicable income tax slab rates upon redemption.
3. **Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) / Secondary Market SGBs**:
   - Issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) on behalf of the Government of India.
   - **Key Advantages**:
     - Earns an additional **2.50% annual interest** paid semi-annually on the initial nominal investment amount.
     - **Capital Gains Tax Exemption**: Complete exemption from capital gains tax if held until the full 8-year maturity.
     - Zero making charges, zero storage costs, and guaranteed purity.
   - SGBs can be purchased in the secondary market through demat accounts at market-determined prices.

## Summary Guidance
For pure investment purposes, paper gold (SGBs or low-cost Gold ETFs) is vastly superior to physical jewellery due to the complete elimination of making charges, purity risk, and recurring locker expenses.
