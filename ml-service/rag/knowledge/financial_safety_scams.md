# Digital Financial Safety and Scam Avoidance

**Author**: MoneyLens team  
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-15  
**Category**: Security & Digital Banking Hygiene  

## Core Principles of Digital Banking Security in India
India's digital payment ecosystem (UPI, IMPS, NEFT, Aadhaar-enabled payments) is among the most advanced in the world. However, cybercriminals and social engineers exploit behavioral vulnerabilities rather than cryptography. Understanding fundamental payment rules prevents financial fraud.

## 1. The Golden Rule of UPI
> **Entering a UPI PIN is strictly and exclusively required to SEND money or deduct funds from your bank account. You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN to RECEIVE money.**
- If a buyer on OLX, an alleged refund agent, or an unknown caller asks you to enter your UPI PIN, scan a QR code, or accept a "Collect Request" to receive funds, it is **100% a scam**.

## 2. One-Time Passwords (OTP) and CVV
- Bank representatives, credit card fraud teams, and RBI officials will **never ask for your OTP, CVV, or card expiry date** over a telephone call, SMS, or WhatsApp message.
- Never share an OTP received via SMS, even if the caller claims your account will be immediately blocked or suspended.

## 3. Screen Sharing and Remote Access Fraud
- Never install remote screen-sharing applications (such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk) at the request of anyone claiming to be customer care support for telecom, courier tracking (e.g., FedEx, India Post), or electricity bill updates.
- Once installed, fraudsters view your banking credentials and OTPs in real time as they arrive on your display.

## 4. Investment Scams and "Guaranteed" Returns
- Be wary of WhatsApp or Telegram groups advertising "algorithmic guaranteed daily returns," "pre-IPO stock tips," or "crypto arbitrage trading bots."
- SEBI regulations strictly prohibit promising guaranteed returns on stock market or equity investments. Legitimate registered financial entities are searchable in the SEBI intermediary directory.

## What to Do If You Suspect Fraud
1. Immediately contact your bank's dedicated 24x7 toll-free fraud helpline to freeze compromised debit/credit cards and UPI access.
2. Dial the National Cyber Crime Helpline at **1930** (operated by the Ministry of Home Affairs) to report financial cyber fraud within the golden hour so banks can freeze illicit fund transfers.
3. Lodge a formal complaint on the official portal: `cybercrime.gov.in`.
