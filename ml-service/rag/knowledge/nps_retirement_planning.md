# National Pension System (NPS) and Retirement Planning Basics

**Author**: MoneyLens team  
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-15  
**Category**: Retirement & Long-Term Planning  

## Overview of the National Pension System (NPS)
The National Pension System (NPS) is a government-sponsored, voluntary, defined-contribution pension scheme regulated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). It is designed to cultivate structured retirement savings across an individual's working lifetime.

## Account Types
1. **Tier 1 (Mandatory Retirement Account)**:
   - Features statutory lock-in until age 60 (or superannuation).
   - Partial withdrawals permitted only under specific emergency conditions (critical illness, child higher education, first home purchase).
   - Generates substantial tax incentives.
2. **Tier 2 (Voluntary Savings Account)**:
   - Flexible liquidity: funds can be deposited and withdrawn at will without exit penalties.
   - Operates similarly to a regular mutual fund, with no special tax deductions (except for central government employees under specific rules).

## Investment Choices & Asset Classes in NPS
Investors can choose how their contributions are divided across four asset classes:
- **Asset Class E (Equity)**: Up to 75% in equity shares of blue-chip companies.
- **Asset Class C (Corporate Bonds)**: Debt instruments issued by public and private corporations.
- **Asset Class G (Government Securities)**: Central and state government bonds with zero credit default risk.
- **Asset Class A (Alternative Assets)**: Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) up to 5%.

Investors can select **Active Choice** (custom percentage selection) or **Auto Choice / Life Cycle Funds** (where equity exposure automatically tapers as the subscriber ages).

## Maturity Rules at Age 60
Upon reaching age 60:
- Up to **60% of the accumulated corpus can be withdrawn as a tax-free lump sum**.
- The remaining minimum **40% must be utilized to purchase an annuity** from an approved Life Insurance company, which provides a regular, lifelong monthly pension to the subscriber.

## Expense Advantage
NPS is globally recognized as one of the lowest-cost pension products in the world, with pension fund manager fees capped at under 0.09% per annum, maximizing net compounded wealth.
