## What is an Index Fund?
An index fund is a mutual fund or Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF) designed to track and replicate the performance of a specific financial market benchmark index (such as the NIFTY 50 or S&P BSE SENSEX). Instead of paying an active fund manager to selectively pick individual stocks or predict short-term market tops and bottoms, an index fund holds the exact constituent stocks in identical weightings as the underlying index.

## Why Passive Index Investing Outperforms Active Funds
1. **Low Expense Ratio (Cost Drag Reduction)**:
   - Active mutual funds in India typically charge between 0.70% and 1.80% in annual total expense ratios (TER).
   - Broad index funds charge minimal expense ratios, often between 0.05% and 0.20%.
   - Over a 20-30 year compounding horizon, saving 1.0% to 1.5% in recurring annual fees compounds into tens of lakhs of additional rupees in net investor returns.

2. **The SPIVA Evidence (S&P Indices Versus Active)**:
   - Empirical studies published globally by S&P Dow Jones Indices consistently demonstrate that over rolling 5, 10, and 15-year windows, **over 70% to 85% of active equity fund managers fail to beat their benchmark index** after accounting for fees and transaction costs.
   - Predicting in advance which active manager will outperform over the coming decade is nearly impossible for individual retail investors.

3. **Elimination of Fund Manager Risk**:
   - Active mutual funds suffer from manager turnover, style drift, and cash drag (holding idle cash during bull runs). Index funds eliminate human manager biases and ensure full market participation.

## Core Pillars for an Indian Passive Investor
- **NIFTY 50 / SENSEX Index Fund**: Captures India's top 50 blue-chip large-cap corporations across banking, IT, energy, FMCG, and automotive.
- **NIFTY Next 50 Index Fund**: Tracks the next 50 emerging large-cap companies with higher growth potential.
- **Low Tracking Error**: When selecting an index fund, prioritize schemes with low tracking error and high AUM (assets under management) for reliable liquidity.
