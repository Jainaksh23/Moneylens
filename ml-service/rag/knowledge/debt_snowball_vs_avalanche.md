# Debt Payoff Strategies: Debt Snowball vs Debt Avalanche

**Author**: MoneyLens team  
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-15  
**Category**: Debt Elimination & Psychology  

## The Challenge of Multiple Debts
Managing multiple liabilities simultaneously (credit card balances, BNPL loans, personal loans, vehicle loans) can cause cognitive overload and high interest bleed. Two structured frameworks help systematically eliminate debt: the Debt Snowball and the Debt Avalanche.

In both strategies, you must continue making the **minimum mandatory payments on all loans** to protect your credit score from defaults, while directing every surplus rupee toward one targeted loan at a time.

## 1. The Debt Avalanche (Mathematically Optimal)
- **Method**: Rank all debts in order from the **highest annual percentage interest rate (APR)** to the lowest, regardless of the principal balance size.
- **Execution**: Direct all extra cash toward paying off the loan with the highest interest rate (e.g., Credit Card debt at 42% APR). Once eliminated, redirect the entire payment stream to the next highest rate (e.g., Personal Loan at 15% APR).
- **Advantage**: Minimizes the total interest paid to lenders over the entire payoff journey and ensures the fastest mathematical path to debt freedom.
- **Drawback**: If the highest-rate debt has a large balance, it may take months or years to see the first debt completely vanish, which requires sustained personal discipline.

## 2. The Debt Snowball (Psychologically Optimal)
- **Method**: Rank all debts in order from the **smallest outstanding balance** to the largest, regardless of interest rates.
- **Execution**: Direct all extra cash toward wiping out the smallest balance loan first. Once paid off, roll that freed payment into attacking the next smallest loan.
- **Advantage**: Delivers quick psychological "wins" and momentum. Seeing account after account drop to zero balance reduces financial stress and motivates continued adherence.
- **Drawback**: You may pay slightly more aggregate interest if a large high-interest loan lingers while small low-interest debts are cleared.

## Which Should You Choose?
- Choose **Debt Avalanche** if you are analytical, strictly motivated by minimizing interest costs, and capable of long-term patience without needing instant feedback.
- Choose **Debt Snowball** if you feel overwhelmed by multiple creditors, need rapid psychological validation, or struggle with financial motivation.
- Any consistent payoff system is infinitely superior to paying only minimum dues.
