# Insurance Fundamentals: Pure Protection vs Investment

**Author**: MoneyLens team  
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-15  
**Category**: Risk & Protection  

## The Fundamental Principle of Insurance
The primary purpose of insurance is risk mitigation and financial catastrophe protection, not capital appreciation or investment returns. Mixing insurance with investment (e.g., Endowment policies, Money-Back plans, Unit Linked Insurance Plans - ULIPs) usually results in neither adequate life coverage nor competitive investment returns due to high mortality charges, distributor commissions, and surrender penalties.

## 1. Pure Term Life Insurance
- **Who needs it?**: Anyone who has financial dependents (spouse, elderly parents, children) who rely on their income. If no one depends on your income, you do not need life insurance yet.
- **Coverage Sizing**: The rule of thumb is **10 to 15 times your annual gross income**, plus the total outstanding amount of liabilities (such as a home loan).
- **Structure**: A pure term plan pays out the agreed sum assured exclusively upon the unfortunate death of the policyholder during the policy term. If the insured survives the term, no maturity benefit is paid. Because there is no investment element, premiums are extraordinarily affordable (e.g., ₹1 Crore cover for a healthy 28-year-old often costs less than ₹10,000–₹14,000 per year).

## 2. Health Insurance (Mediclaim)
- **Do Not Rely Solely on Employer Corporate Cover**: Corporate group health policies terminate immediately upon resignation, layoff, or retirement, and often feature co-payment restrictions.
- **Individual or Family Floater**: Secure a comprehensive standalone retail health insurance policy early in life when you have no pre-existing illnesses.
- **Key Features to Verify**:
  - No room-rent capping (or single private AC room allowance).
  - Minimal disease-specific sub-limits.
  - Restoration / recharge benefit for unrelated illnesses.
  - Pre- and post-hospitalisation expense coverage (60/180 days).
  - Super Top-Up policy: A cost-effective way to scale coverage from ₹5-10 Lakhs to ₹50 Lakhs or ₹1 Crore with high deductibles.

## Summary Strategy
Keep protection and investment strictly segregated:
- Buy pure Term Life Insurance for dependents.
- Buy comprehensive standalone Health Insurance for medical security.
- Invest the money saved through low insurance premiums in low-cost index funds or PPF/NPS for long-term compounding.
